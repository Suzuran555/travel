\begin{abstract}
We describe the Phase-2 harness of team \emph{Antarctic penguins} for the
ChinaTravel Travel Planning Challenge. The system is neuro-symbolic: a served
Qwen3.6-27B is used \emph{only} for natural-language-to-DSL translation, and
everything downstream --- constraint canonicalization, symbolic search, and
soft-metric enrichment --- is deterministic and mechanically verified against
the \emph{generated} constraints, never the hidden oracle. Phase~1 finished at
$100.000$ on the public leaderboard. For Phase~2 we adapted the system to the
substantially tightened official evaluator (per-day meal-type limits,
cross-activity chronology, transport--position consistency, and pass-rate
coupled preference scoring) with three gated repair passes
(\texttt{deoverlap}, \texttt{fixspace}, \texttt{mustpoi}); on the 100
familiarization queries under fully honest live-NL conditions
(oracle fields stripped, real Qwen3.6-27B), these passes lift Overall from
$80.36$ to $\mathbf{93.54}$ (FPR $76\!\to\!93$, C-LPR $82.7\!\to\!98.3$).
The harness answers the organizers' fixed entry command, honors the
5-hour/100-query budget with per-query caps and a deterministic fallback,
survives oracle-free held-out data by construction, and was verified
end-to-end against a protocol-exact SGLang simulator with zero environment
overrides.
\end{abstract}

\section{System Architecture}

The pipeline uses the LLM only where language understanding is irreplaceable;
all downstream stages are deterministic:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{NL$\to$DSL translation} (Qwen3.6-27B via the organizers'
SGLang endpoint, thinking disabled, temperature $0$, fixed seed). Prompts are
hardened against failure modes measured on Qwen itself (e.g.\ a stock few-shot
example that induced invented room constraints; fixing it alone moved measured
translation fidelity from $46\%$ to ${\sim}89\%$). Mechanical verifiers and
normalizers reject or repair malformed DSL with no oracle access.
\item \textbf{DSL canonicalization}: constraint literals, aliases, and quoting
are normalized so the symbolic layer sees a stable language regardless of LLM
phrasing variance.
\item \textbf{Symbolic planning} (\emph{UrbanTripOptimizedV6}): phased bundle
search over intercity transport, accommodation, attractions, and meals with
geographic anchoring, DFS memoization, budget-drop repair, and a travel-day
timing bias (rewarding early arrival / late departure; worth DAV $+4.4$,
DDR $+9.6$ on fresh plans in Phase 1).
\item \textbf{Gated enrichment battery} (15 stages): every edit is kept only
if the plan still passes schema, commonsense, and the \emph{generated} hard
constraints, and a soft metric strictly improves with none regressing. The
gate never reads oracle fields --- which the formal held-out data does not
contain anyway.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Adapting to the Tightened Phase-2 Evaluator}
\label{sec:adapt}

Between the phases, the official evaluator gained per-day meal-type limits
(one breakfast/lunch/dinner each), cross-activity chronology (no overlaps;
transports must depart after the previous activity ends), strict
transport--position consistency validated against the environment, and
pass-rate-coupled preference scoring. Plans and strategies tuned against the
older evaluator regress sharply: our Phase-1-style output lost $31.5\%$ of
plans to newly enforced checks. We answered with three repair passes, all
gated on the official evaluators over generated constraints:

\paragraph{\texttt{deoverlap} --- chronology rethreading.}
Time-only, position-preserving repair: a late-starting activity is shifted to
the previous activity's end (duration kept, clamped at 24:00), and whole
transport chains are time-shifted so they depart after the previous activity
and arrive by the activity start. Respects the evaluator's semantics exactly:
positions are tracked \emph{across} days while chronology resets \emph{per}
day.

\paragraph{\texttt{fixspace} --- transport-chain rebuilding.}
Where a leg is anchored to the wrong POI (classically: the return-flight
transfer departing from an attraction instead of the hotel), relabeling is
insufficient because the evaluator validates each leg's price, distance, and
duration against the environment. The pass therefore \emph{rebuilds} the chain
with the environment's own routing (\texttt{goto}), probing
metro/walk/taxi and time-shifting the probe to arrive exactly at the activity
start. Adoption is gated: commonsense must flip to pass and the hard verdict
must not degrade. Measured effect: MacEPR $84\!\to\!99$,
C-LPR $82.7\!\to\!95.5$ ($+5.6$ Overall).

\paragraph{\texttt{mustpoi} --- required-POI recovery.}
Parses required-POI targets from the \emph{generated} DSL --- in every dialect
the live model emits (\lstinline|{X}<=set|, \lstinline|'X' in set|,
\lstinline|set=={X,Y}|, \lstinline|not({X}&set)|, escaped quotes) --- and
applies a family of gated fixers: check-in window shifts and hotel rebooking
(with DB-correct price/room fields and rebuilt legs); hotel-meal insertion,
shifting, and relocation; named-restaurant relocation and insertion (never
cannibalizing another required target); attraction insertion into the evening
slot \emph{or any midday gap}; cuisine and attraction-type set membership
including forbidden-type removal; intercity return-leg swap to a required
train/airplane (skipped when already satisfied); and meal-budget reduction
(cheapest relocation, then zero-cost hotel-meal conversion). Two mechanisms
proved decisive: (i) \emph{group-level gating} --- multi-item requirements
such as $\{A,B\}\subseteq$ \texttt{name\_set} are applied as a batch and gated
as a whole, since no single insert can flip them; and (ii)
\emph{transport-mode awareness} --- banned modes are parsed from the
constraints (a no-walk query also bans metro, whose composite legs contain
walking) and every insert restricts its legs accordingly, sparing inner-city
budgets with walk-first probing where walking is allowed.

Across four diagnostic rounds this recovered $15$ of the $22$ hard-logic
failures (Table~\ref{tab:trajectory}). A finding worth emphasizing: on this
benchmark the translations for all $22$ failing plans were already
near-verbatim correct --- the planner \emph{knew} the constraints and violated
them --- so the recoverable error mass had moved from translation to
constraint-aware plan repair.

\section{Harness Engineering}

\textbf{Entry point.} The organizers run
\lstinline|python agent_env/scripts/solve_script_with_harness.py --method <m> --split <s>|.
Our package extends the official harness example with a custom in-process
\texttt{tpcagent} runner (five minimal edits to the entry script plus one new
file); \lstinline|--method|/\lstinline|--split| always override the shipped
configuration, and no split name, query list, count, or method is hard-coded.

\textbf{Budget.} The 5-hour/100-query limit is enforced with a global soft
deadline (4h50m) plus a per-query cap (170\,s); on timeout or any error a
deterministic database-only fallback plan is emitted, so a result file always
exists and one slow query cannot abort the run.

\textbf{Oracle-free robustness.} The formal held-out data carries no oracle
fields, which makes the harness-internal evaluator raise
\texttt{KeyError('hard\_logic\_py')}. The result file is written \emph{before}
that call and is the actual deliverable, so the call is guarded: the run
continues regardless.

\textbf{Verification.} Beyond unit and end-to-end tests, we verified the
exact organizer invocation: the shipped ZIP, freshly unzipped, with
\emph{zero} environment overrides, against a protocol-exact SGLang simulator
(\texttt{scripts/mock\_sglang.py}: same port, OpenAI surface, strict
\texttt{Qwen3.6-27B} model-name check, thinking disabled) backed by a local
Qwen3.6 --- producing a valid plan with zero parse failures. An
oracle-stripped copy of the familiarization split
(\texttt{scripts/make\_heldout\_sim.py}) reproduces held-out conditions
locally; all 100 result files are produced without interruption.

\section{Findings for the Community}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \emph{Evaluator co-evolution is the hidden variable.} A 100.0-scoring
Phase-1 system lost a third of its plans to the tightened checks; strategies
must be re-validated whenever the evaluator moves.
\item \emph{Generated-DSL dialects matter.} The live model expresses the same
constraint in at least four syntactic dialects; any component that parses
generated DSL must handle all of them (and escaped quotes in POI names such
as \emph{Sizhao'er}).
\item \emph{Repair must be environment-grounded.} Post-hoc edits that merely
relabel transports fail leg-validation; repairs must rebuild legs through the
environment APIs.
\item \emph{Gate granularity must match constraint arity.} Per-step gating
starves multi-item set requirements; batch application with group-level
gating fixes them.
\item \emph{Preference--pass coupling changes optimal play.} Once failed
plans count as zero in DAV/ATT/DDR, every recovered plan pays triple; soft
metrics are no longer separable from feasibility.
\end{enumerate}

During development we also reported nine upstream evaluator/data issues to the
organizers (e.g.\ an AST whitelist rejecting \texttt{break}/\texttt{continue}
used by official constraint programs, a \texttt{people\_number} NameError, and
conflicting POI-alias tables).

\section{Reproducibility and Disclosures}

The submission is a self-contained superset of the official harness example:
one entry command, pinned sampling (temperature $0$, fixed seed), volatile
counters stripped from outputs, databases included, and the verification
tooling (SGLang mock, held-out simulator) shipped inside the package.
Development used AI assistance for code authoring and diagnostics; all
scoring-relevant behavior is deterministic code reviewed into the repository.
Oracle constraint fields were used only for offline scoring during
development, never by the agent at inference; the inference path strips them
defensively and the formal data does not contain them.

\section{Limitations}

Seven familiarization plans remain unrecovered: morning-cascade windows
(a required hotel-breakfast window conflicting with an already-departed
morning leg), no-walk queries with tight inner-city budgets (taxi-only
repairs exceed the allowance), compound multi-set cases, and one plan whose
base chronology cannot be rethreaded without re-planning. These require
planner-level rescheduling rather than post-hoc repair. Translation fidelity
under the organizers' BF16 serving may differ slightly from our local
stand-ins; the pipeline's verifiers and the repair battery are designed to
absorb such variance.
